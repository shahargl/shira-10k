THE RULES THAT MATTER

Train the runner.
Protect the knee.

01

Easy means easy

Most kilometres should allow full-sentence conversation. Slowing down is part of the program.

02

Speed stays controlled

Quicker segments should always feel repeatable, never like an all-out test.

03

Pain changes the plan

Stop for sharp pain, swelling, limping, or discomfort that increases during a run.

The 24-hour ruleIf discomfort is worse later that day or the next morning, reduce the next run by 20–30% and remove fast segments.

THE PROGRESSION

Build. Peak. Freshen up.

Distance rises gradually, peaks once, then steps down before race day.

OCTOBER 28
